# Storage

Batch 13 (Nov 22), from Richie's list of what MoveCrew was missing. Moving companies keep customers' things in
their warehouse between moves: the new place isn't ready yet, the closing slipped, a long-distance delivery comes
later. The things go in vaults (wooden crates, about 5 by 7 by 8 feet), billed every month, and come out on a
delivery move.

**Setup › Options › Storage** (on to start, under Office). The vaults and the rate are in **Setup › Storage**.

## The storage account

A customer's storage is an account of its own, a job like any other: payments, pay links, the customer app, texts
and the activity all work on it the same way. Its status is **In storage** (a dashed pill), so the Board, Today's
jobs, the crew's screens, Sales and the reports on moves never count it as a move.

- **The vaults** it has, from Setup › Storage. A vault can be in one account at a time.
- **The rate**: a vault a month ($150 to start, from Setup). Each account keeps its own rate.
- **The bills**: the first comes the day it goes in, then the same day of each month (the 28th at the latest, so
  a move-in on the 31st is billed on the 28th after that). "Storage Oct 13 to Nov 12: 2 vaults at $150". A bill
  is made on its day by the office's phone or computer, and every office phone makes the same one, so nothing is
  billed twice. Changing the vaults or the rate changes the next bills; the ones made stay.
- **What's owed** is the bills less what's paid, oldest bill first. A bill is **past due** 10 days after it comes.
- A **handling fee** (Setup › Storage, $0 to start) goes on the account once, when it's made.

## Put in storage

On a move's job sheet (quoted, booked, running, finished or paid), the **Storage** part: tap the vaults (the free
ones show), check the rate and **Goes in (the first bill)** (the move's last day, to start), then **Put in storage:
2 vaults, $300 a month**. The move then says "Into storage after this move: Vault 3, Vault 4" and opens the account,
and the crew's card for the move says **Into storage: Vault 3, Vault 4** under the addresses.

**Someone already in storage** (Setup › Storage): for customers who were in the warehouse before MoveCrew. Their
name, phone and email, the rate, the vaults, and the **Next bill** (the bills start that day; none before).

## The account's job sheet

- At the top: **Billed**, **Paid**, and **Owed** (**Past due** once a bill is 10 days old, or **Paid up**), with
  **Collect payment** when something's owed. No Edit quote, no Schedule, no crew.
- **Storage account** (open to start): since when, the move it came from (**Open it**), the vaults (tap to add or
  take one off; the last one stays), **A vault a month**, **In storage since**, and **First bill** until the first
  one comes. "Billed on the 13th of each month. Next: Sun, Dec 13, $300." Then each bill: **Paid**, **Part paid**,
  **Owed** or **Past due**.
- **Add a charge or credit**: a late fee, a credit for a box taken out early. It goes on the account on its day,
  and payments go to the oldest first.
- **Deliver from storage**: a move quote from the warehouse, with what's stored on it ("From storage: Vault 3,
  Vault 4" on the crew's card). It opens to finish: put in where it goes and the day, then save or book it. When that
  move starts, the account comes **out of storage** that day: no bills from then on (the first month stays), and it's
  **Complete** while something's owed, then **Paid**.
- **Taken out another way: close it** (tap twice): out of storage today, the same way.
- A closed account can be **Put back in storage** while its vaults are free.

## The Storage screen

Richie, Nov 22: "Why is there not a storage tab that tells you what is available and what is taken. Who is paid and
who owes." **Storage** is in More (and in the side list on a computer), for the owner and dispatch, while Options ›
Storage is on or anyone has been in storage.

- **At the top:** vaults free ("6 Vaults free, of 12"), Taken, Owed (red when something is) and Storage a month.
  Dispatch sees the last two only with Options › Dispatch sees revenue on.
- **Vaults:** every vault as a tile: Free (dashed), or the customer's name and where they stand, green Paid up, orange
  "$420 owed", red "$600 past due". Tap a taken one to open that customer's account. A legend says what the
  colours mean.
- **Who's in storage:** past due first (the oldest first), then owing, then paid up, and anyone out of storage who
  still owes: the name, a month, the vaults, since when and the next bill, and Paid up or what they owe. **All**,
  **Owe** and **Paid up** filter it.
- For the owner, a button to Setup › Storage at the bottom. Jobs › In storage has **Storage ›** to it.

## Where it shows

- **Storage** (above), and **Jobs**: an **In storage** chip. Each account: "Since Oct 13 · Vault 3, Vault 4 · $300 a month", $300/mo, and
  "$300 owed" or "$600 past due".
- **Today**: "Storage bill past due: Lena Wu", "$600 owed · since Tue, Oct 13" (three or more on one row, which
  opens Jobs › In storage).
- **Reports › Storage**: who's in storage now, the vaults in use of how many, a month's storage, what's owed and
  what's past due, and what was billed in the dates picked.
- **Job history**: a closed, paid account, the day after.
- **The customer app**: "In storage since Tuesday, October 13", the vaults at the warehouse (Setup › Storage's
  address, or the shop's), $300 a month and the next bill; **Pay your storage bill** with what's owed (Google Pay,
  Apple Pay or a card, the same pay page as a move; no tip); the next bill; **When you want your things: call or text
  to set a delivery day**; and **Your storage bills** with what's in storage. Once it's out: "Out of storage".

## Setup › Storage

The rate a vault a month, the handling fee, the warehouse's address (blank: the shop's), and the vaults: 12 to
start ("Vault 1" to "Vault 12"), each named as the company likes ("A-1"), added, or removed while free. A vault in
use shows whose it is.

## Not in this batch

- The bills aren't texted or emailed to the customer on their own yet: they see them and pay in the customer app,
  and the office can send a pay link from the account. Monthly bill texts and emails come with messages (batch 14).
- Storage by the cubic foot or by the pallet, late fees, and partial pickups (some things out, the rest stays) are
  done by hand for now: the rate, the vaults, and Add a charge or credit on the account.
- Card on file charged every month (autopay) comes with payments (batch 15).
